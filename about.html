About Junction 41

Who runs the trains, what is on offer, and who built this website.

nested liststext formattingsub / supdiv profile card

Junction 41 is a rail travel service built around one idea: the journey is the destination. We run six scenic lines across India, each with a fixed timetable, honest fares and a window seat for every booked passenger.

Our onboard water is filtered to the last drop (H2O, nothing else), and every coach is cleaned to a standard of 103 checks a month.

What we offer

  • Scenic lines
    • Nilgiri Whistle
    • Valley Arrow
    • Pink City Limited
  • Comfort
    • Window-seat booking
    • Sleeper berths
    • Chai at dawn
  • Support
    • Special assistance
